## Logo propio
Generado con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes, fondo transparente. Archivo: static/images/logo-graffiti.png.

Prompt: Create a polished raster graffiti wordmark for an educational interactive Python computer-vision notebook website inspired by Black Clover. Exact text: AULAVISIÓN, smaller GRIMOIRE LAB. Angular hand-painted ivory lettering, acid lime highlights, restrained crimson strokes, black ink outlines, paint splashes and small clover motif. Dark fantasy meets street art, compact wide layout, transparent background. No characters or extra words.
